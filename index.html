07 FAQ

Quick answers, before you ask.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins get the next open station, usually within 10 minutes on weekdays. For weekends, VIP bays, and tournaments, book via WhatsApp the day before.

Can I bring my own keyboard, mouse, or controller?

Yes. Plug-and-play USB and Bluetooth peripherals are fine. We've seen everything from custom pads to sim wheels. The drivers are pre-installed for most common brands.

Are snacks and drinks allowed at the rig?

Closed-bottle drinks and dry snacks only. We've got ramen, energy drinks, and proper coffee at the counter. Greasy food and the gear don't mix, and the staff will politely ask you to step away.

Do you host birthday parties and squad events?

Yes. VIP bays seat up to 4, and we can string two bays for 8-player nights. We also run private tournaments with custom brackets. Message us on WhatsApp with the headcount and date.

Is there an age limit?

All ages are welcome during the day. After 9pm, under-16s need a parent or guardian on the floor. M-rated titles are restricted regardless of the time of day.

What if I just want to try it out first?

Grab a Quick Match hour on Standard for ₹120. If you want the Pro tier for a test drive, ask at the desk and we'll route you to the next open Pro rig.
